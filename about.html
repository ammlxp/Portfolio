About

I’m a graphic designer based in London with a strong interest in typography and bookmaking. I enjoy researching underexplored themes, often rooted in history or culture.

My practice brings together a range of disciplines, including letterpress, coding, calligraphy, and bookbinding, which I use to create work that asks questions and encourages reflection. I value collaboration and aim to produce work that connects meaningfully with people and communities, placing them at the heart of the process.
