Flaxman
(Type Revival Project)

This is a manifesto about the origins and essence of krump, a style of street dance. It is meant to portray power through movement and break stereotypes of how street dance is often visually characterised. I used typography in a systematic way with intentional moments where this is broken to represent the rhythm of the dance. Alongside this submission there is a Specification document outlining every design decision made. This is available upon request.
